# Business Model

## Business Thesis

Wanderless Laos starts as a B2B2C decision-support layer for local travel operators and guide marketplaces, rather than trying to acquire all tourists directly from day one.

## Customer Segments

1. Local tour operators
2. Boutique hotels / concierge desks
3. Destination-management companies
4. Guide associations
5. Travel marketplaces

## Revenue Model

**Primary:** Commission on successful guide bookings (15–18%)

**Secondary:** SaaS dashboard for operators; featured guide placements (governed carefully); data insights for operators (aggregated, privacy-safe)

## Why B2B2C First

- Lower CAC than pure consumer app
- Operators already have guide supply
- Hotels/operators already have traveler demand
- Product can prove conversion uplift before scaling

## Unit Economics — Assumptions Clearly Labeled

| Metric                |                                Assumption | Why It Matters          |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------: | ----------------------- |
| Average booking value |                          $80–120 per tour | Drives commission pool  |
| Commission rate       |                                    15–18% | Core revenue take-rate  |
| Conversion uplift     |          Pilot target: +15–20% vs catalog | Validates product value |
| Guide onboarding cost |                           $0–50 per guide | Supply-side scalability |
| CAC                   | $15–30 per tourist via hotel partnerships | Must be tested in pilot |

_All metrics are assumptions or pilot targets, not validated operating metrics._

## Pilot Plan

**90-day pilot:**

- 20–30 guides
- 3–5 operator/hotel partners
- 100–300 tourist matching sessions
- Track conversion, cancellation, satisfaction, guide acceptance, repeat/referral intent

## Success Metrics

- Match-to-booking conversion rate
- Booking cancellation rate
- Tourist satisfaction (post-tour)
- Guide acceptance rate
- Average response time
- Repeat/referral intent
- Safety incident escalation rate

## A+ Investor Logic

The wedge is not itinerary generation. The wedge is compatibility data. Over time, Wanderless can build a proprietary matching dataset linking traveler preferences, guide attributes, itinerary context, and satisfaction outcomes.
