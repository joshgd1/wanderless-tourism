# Market & Problem — A+ Rubric Evidence

## 1. Core Problem

WanderLess is solving decision friction in local guided travel.

Travellers visiting Laos often do not simply need more itinerary options. They need confidence that:

- the guide matches their interests,
- the guide's communication style fits them,
- the pace and activity level are suitable,
- the group is compatible,
- the experience is trustworthy,
- and the proposed plan is safe enough for their comfort level.

This is a compatibility and trust problem, not just an itinerary-generation problem.

## 3. Laos / Luang Prabang Beachhead Rationale

Luang Prabang is a focused beachhead because it is a tourism-dense heritage destination where traveller satisfaction depends heavily on local interpretation, cultural context, language ability, trust, and pace fit. Starting with one city is more credible than claiming a global travel marketplace from day one. The Laos wedge gives WanderLess a narrower and more differentiated entry point than competing immediately in saturated destinations such as Vientiane, Vang Vieng, Bali, or Singapore.

| Beachhead Criterion  | Why Luang Prabang Fits                                                        |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| High guide relevance | Heritage, temples, food, nature, and local culture require interpretation     |
| Trust need           | Travellers need confidence in guide reliability and safety style              |
| Focused geography    | Easier to build guide supply density than a country-wide launch               |
| Differentiation      | Less crowded than major Southeast Asian mass-tourism hubs                     |
| Expansion logic      | Can later expand to Vientiane, Vang Vieng, Pakse, Champasak, and Luang Namtha |

## 4. Target Customer

**Primary tourist segment:** English-speaking free independent travellers aged 25–45 visiting Laos, especially solo travellers, couples, and small groups seeking cultural, food, nature, heritage, and local-neighbourhood experiences.

**Primary guide/supplier segment:** Licensed, credentialed, or reputation-backed local guides and small tourism operators in Luang Prabang who need better demand acquisition and differentiation beyond price and generic star ratings.

**Economic buyer:** Tourist pays through booking commission embedded in marketplace transaction. Guide accepts commission because WanderLess provides demand, qualification, and higher-fit leads.

## 7. Why Now

Generic AI itinerary generation is becoming commoditised. The next defensible layer is not producing more travel plans; it is helping travellers select the right guide, group, and local experience with confidence. WanderLess uses AI/ML as a decision-support layer around human local expertise.
